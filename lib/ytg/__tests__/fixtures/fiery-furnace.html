The story of Daniel and his friends who didn’t bow before Nebuchadnezzar’s idol on pain of death is one of true inspiration and dedication to their faith in God. This deck tells that story and the overall story of Judah’s captivity in Babylon. The offense has strong banding heroes that are backed up by powerful negate enhancements, while the defense capitalizes on powerful capture abilities.  The Daniel offense is currently a meta offense in Redemption, so you don’t want to miss this one!
